Audio is uploaded with https://tus.io/protocols/resumable-upload[tus 1.0.0], with the extensions `creation` and
`termination`, so a large file survives a broken connection.
The upload's id is then given to the GraphQL mutation that makes it a track (`trackCreate`) or replaces a track's audio (`trackReplaceAudio`).
An upload that is not used is deleted after 24 hours.
Only its owner can reach an upload.

Send chunks of at most 50 MB: Cloudflare, in front of the production server, refuses larger requests.
