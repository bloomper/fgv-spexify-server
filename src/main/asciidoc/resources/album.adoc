[[download]]
== Download

Every track of the album the caller may reach, as a zip streamed while it is read, without compression (audio does not compress).

operation::album-download-get[snippets='security,request-headers,http-request,response-headers']

A signed link to the zip, like a xref:track.adoc#link[track's], holds the same tracks as the caller would get above.

operation::album-download-link-get[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-headers,http-request,http-response,response-fields']

[[cover]]
== Cover

The album's own cover, else the poster of its spex from Spexregister, else `404 Not Found`.

operation::album-cover-get[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-headers,http-request,response-headers']

[[cover_upload]]
== Cover upload

Replaces the album's cover.
The image type is read from the file, not from its name; anything but JPEG, PNG and WebP is `400 Bad Request`.
The answer is the album, as in GraphQL.

operation::album-cover-post[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-parts,request-headers,http-request,response-fields,http-response']
